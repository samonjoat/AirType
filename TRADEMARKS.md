The AirType name, logo, application icon, and other distinctive AirType brand
elements identify the official project and releases maintained at
`https://github.com/samonjoat/AirType`. They are not licensed under the GNU
Affero General Public License.

This policy does not limit any permission granted by `AGPL-3.0-only` for the
software itself. It governs only use of AirType branding and does not prevent
truthful statements about the origin of the software.

## Uses That Do Not Require Permission

- Refer to the official AirType project, quote its name, or link to it.
- State truthfully that software is based on, compatible with, or derived from AirType.
- Redistribute an unmodified official release and identify it as AirType when
  the files and checksums match an official release and the source location is clear.
- Use the name in commentary, reviews, tutorials, academic work, and community discussion.

These uses must not imply sponsorship, endorsement, partnership, or official
status when none exists.

## Uses That Require Prior Written Permission

- Use AirType or a confusingly similar term as the name of a modified product,
  hosted service, business, domain, package, installer, or application listing.
- Use the AirType logo, application icon, or visual identity for a modified distribution.
- Claim that a fork, modified build, or third-party service is an official AirType release.
- Use AirType branding in advertising or merchandise in a way that suggests endorsement.

## Modified Distributions

Modified distributions must use a distinct product name, logo, and application
identity. They may state that they are derived from AirType, must identify their
modifications as required by the AGPL, and should link to the official project.

Trademark questions or permission requests may be submitted privately through
the [AirType security advisory form](https://github.com/samonjoat/AirType/security/advisories/new)
with a title beginning `Trademark request`.
